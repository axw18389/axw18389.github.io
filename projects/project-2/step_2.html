Drawing Arms

Connect the dots

Once you got the basic layout done, you will then want to draw large ovals connect from one dot to the next. But when you get to the last dot, turn the hexagon into the shape of a hand with a almost, triangle shape at the top. This will then give you a rough idea of what kind of shape you need to get done and help with the next step.
